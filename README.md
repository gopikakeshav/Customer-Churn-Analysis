# Maven Music
## ✦ Discovering ways to increase subscription using Python EDA 

## Objective:
To discover the factors that help predict customer cancellations at Maven Music.

## Tools:
* Jupyter Notebook
* Pandas, numpy, Seaborn

## Data source:
Maven Analytics

## Business Problem:
~(Courtesy Maven Analytics)~
The store is facing decrease in revenue due to increasing subscription cancellations. 
The store wants identify why customers cancel and how to increase customer sign ups.

## Analysis:
Find the .ipynb [here](Maven_Music_eda.ipynb) 
1. Try to better understand the customers who cancelled.
    1. How long were they customers before they cancelled?
    2. What % of customers who had discounts cancelled?
    3. Visualize the cancellation rate. 
2. Try to understand the customers listening history
    1. How many listening sessions did each customer have in the past 3 months
    2. What were the most popular genres that customers listened to
3. Visualize the relationships
    1. What are the observations
    2. What are some of the good predictors of customer cancellations

## Observations:
Some of the good predictors of cancellation are:
1. Discounts.
2. No. of listening sessions.
3. Percentage of Pop music listened.
